# MatchFetch

Fetch, browse, and explore your AncestryDNA match data.

## Features

- **Match List** - View all your AncestryDNA matches in a clean card layout with photos, names, and relationship info
- **Ethnicity Estimates** - See percentages broken down by macro region with confidence ranges, sorted by value
- **Genetic Journeys** - Explore your journeys in a hierarchical tree view with strength badges (Very Likely, Likely, Possible)
- **Match Detail** - Click any match to dive into their regions and journeys, organized in tabs
- **Multiple Kits** - Switch between your DNA kits, all stored separately
- **Persistent Storage** - Data is saved in your browser so you don&#39;t need to re-fetch every time
- **Export** - Download all your data as JSON anytime

## Installation

1. Download or clone this repo
2. Open Chrome and go to `chrome://extensions`
3. Enable **Developer mode** (toggle in the top right)
4. Click **Load unpacked** and select the `matchfetch_ext` folder
5. The MatchFetch icon will appear in your toolbar

## Usage

1. Click the MatchFetch icon in your browser toolbar
2. Click **Fetch Tests** to load your AncestryDNA kits
3. Select a kit, then click **Fetch** to pull up all matches
4. Click any match card to open the detail page
5. Use the **Export** button in the top bar to save your data

## Why MatchFetch?

Ancestry shows you one match at a time. MatchFetch pulls all your matches into one place so you can see everyone at a glance, sorted how you want. Click any match to see their ethnicity breakdown and genetic journeys without hopping between pages. It saves everything in your browser, so once you fetch, your data is always there even after you close the tab.
